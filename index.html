About me

Hello, I'm Aarath.

I created Sprout to help people study and prepare for the big moments in their lives. I'm a 7th grader and a dedicated self-learner, and outside of my studies I enjoy soccer, biking, and spending time with friends. I'm still figuring things out along the way, but I hope that one day I can accomplish something meaningful.

When I'm not studying, I'm usually programming. I love coding and building things like Sprout, because it lets me turn an idea into something real that other people can use. I've always loved math, and I'm curious about how things work on the inside, whether it's a computer, a machine, or the human body. I'm also a huge soccer fan. I cheer for FC Barcelona and watch as many of their matches as I can, and I love playing the game myself with my friends.

My dream is to become a neurosurgeon, motivated by a simple purpose: to help people. I'm curious about medical internships, math, science, history, and just about everything else. I've taken part in several local events, including MathCounts and National History Day, and I'm now preparing for bigger ones.

I hope you enjoy Sprout.
